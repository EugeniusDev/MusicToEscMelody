From score to startup

Bring a digital score

Export MusicXML (.musicxml or .mxl) from MuseScore, Dorico, Sibelius or Finale. Standard MIDI type 0 and 1 also work. For a PDF or scan, use a separate optical music recognition tool, correct the notes, then export MusicXML.

Choose a short phrase

Select a part and voice, choose the excerpt, then set tempo and transpose. Each ESC plays one pitch at a time. The piano roll and preview show the actual quantized, trimmed export.

Copy to the configurator

RTTTL is intended for AM32 and Bluejay melody editors. Firmware and configurator versions vary: check that your editor accepts RTTTL and that its encoded melody fits. The app checks the 128-byte encoding used by bluejay-rtttl-parse 2.0.2. Preview in your configurator to confirm compatibility. Remove propellers before working on powered ESCs.
